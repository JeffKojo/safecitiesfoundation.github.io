Privacy notice

What we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Safe Cities Foundation is a registered non-governmental organisation based in Kumasi, Ghana. This notice explains how we handle information submitted through safecitiesfoundation.org.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. If you complete the contact form, we receive the name, email address, organisation, country, enquiry type and message you enter. If you subscribe to updates, we receive the email address you provide. We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive personal information through this website.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies on this site.

Why we collect it

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your enquiry and, where a partnership or collaboration follows, to continue that correspondence. Newsletter addresses are used only to send occasional updates about our work, a few times a year rather than weekly marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. Access is restricted to authorised Safe Cities Foundation staff who sign in with individual accounts. Google's own privacy terms apply to the hosting infrastructure.

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the fonts are requested. No other third party receives your data from this website.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it remains relevant to an ongoing or prospective relationship, and delete enquiries that lead nowhere once they are no longer useful. Newsletter addresses are kept until you ask to be removed.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You may ask to be removed from our update list at any time. Write to info@safecitiesfoundation.org and we will action the request.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Our school-zone work involves photographing public infrastructure; where pupils appear in project photography we obtain permission from the school concerned.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the date below.
