Our story

People-centred cities, built on evidence

Registered NGO · Kumasi, Ghana
Established 2024 with a mandate for people-centred urban transformation, serving the wider African continent.

Safe Cities Foundation works at the intersection of urban mobility, spatial planning and civic advocacy. We believe the future of African cities must be shaped by the people who live in them, not by car-centric legacies inherited from elsewhere.

As a new organisation, we bring fresh energy and evidence-based methods to one of the continent's most urgent challenges: how do we build cities that are safe, sustainable and genuinely inclusive for every resident, regardless of income, gender, age or ability?

From the streets of Kumasi to communities across sub-Saharan Africa, we partner with governments, development institutions, community groups and residents to reshape the urban experience through research, advocacy and co-design.

Our approach

Three pillars driving change

Every initiative is grounded in these three mutually reinforcing principles.

01

Advocacy

We engage government, city authorities and international development partners to champion policies that prioritise people over vehicles: pedestrian infrastructure and cycle lanes to integrated public transport and inclusive zoning.

Policy & governance
02

Co-design

Residents know their neighbourhoods best. We facilitate participatory processes that put community knowledge, lived experience and cultural context at the heart of every project, from market redesigns to transit corridors.

Community-led
03

Evidence

From GIS mapping and pedestrian counts to commuter surveys and road-safety analytics, we build the data infrastructure African cities need to make smarter, fairer planning decisions grounded in local realities.

Our people

A small team of practitioners

Safe Cities Foundation is run by a team based in Kumasi: programme coordinators who deliver the interventions, and research officers who produce the evidence behind them.

We are deliberately small. Every person on the team works on live projects rather than administering them from a distance.

Governance

Accountable by design

We are a registered non-governmental organisation in Ghana, operating with transparency toward our partners, funders and the communities we serve.

Every project we deliver is documented publicly: what was installed, where, when and with whom. Our research is published so city authorities, academics and other organisations can build on it. Where we work with institutional partners such as KNUST and UN-Habitat, their frameworks and reporting standards govern the work alongside our own.

We are a young organisation and we report accordingly: our impact figures reflect completed work, not projections.
